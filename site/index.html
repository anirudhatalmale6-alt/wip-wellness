A place to begin

Growing your relationship with yourself — and everyone else.

Whether you are dealing with anxiety, stress, depression, life transitions, family, or other issues, we are here to help you grow your relationship with yourself and others.

Work in Progress Wellness is a small, unhurried practice. There is no waiting-room shuffle and no script. Sessions are run at your pace, in a space where nothing you bring is too much or too messy. You set the direction; I bring the training, the structure and a steady presence while you find your footing.

Sessions are available in person at our Portland office or online by telehealth, anywhere in Oregon.

Change is possible

You know yourself better than anyone.

If you desire change and are ready to take an active part in your treatment, I am here to help. Whether in person or online, you will be in a safe and accepting therapeutic environment that facilitates growth, insight and change.

Your therapist

Grae Rose

Professional Counselor Associate

I approach therapy from a holistic perspective, taking into account all the thoughts, beliefs and experiences that have shaped and influenced your life.

We will work together to recognise and understand patterns in your life with the goal of strengthening relationships, identifying support systems and harvesting your innate strengths and resources — allowing you to make the changes you desire.

I hold a Master's Degree in Psychology, Marriage & Family Therapy from Golden Gate University in San Francisco and am trained in a variety of therapeutic models, including person-centred therapy, narrative therapy, mindfulness work, cognitive behavioural therapy and motivational interviewing. I am a staunch LGBTQ+ ally.
